The referral terms, in plain language.

Effective August 31, 2026. All rewards are in Canadian dollars.

A reward for the referrer

You receive $50 CAD for an eligible officer-ticket referral or $20 CAD for an eligible camera-ticket referral. The referred driver receives no referral discount. They can still qualify independently for the pro driver discount.

Past clients and registered portal users

Sign in or create a free Fabsy portal account to get your own code. There is no limit on eligible referrals or total rewards.

30 days, last referral wins

A referral link or code must be captured before payment. Attribution lasts 30 days, and the most recent valid referral replaces an earlier one. The driver can enter your code at step 3 of intake if the link was not captured.

Payment and an accepted file

A referral qualifies only after the driver's Stripe payment settles and Fabsy accepts the Alberta file into its service pipeline. The seven-day period begins after both conditions are met. Fabsy pays approved rewards by Interac e-transfer, subject to eligibility checks and complete payout details.

No fleet-account or self-referral payouts

Referred fleet accounts are excluded. Referrer and referred driver cannot share an email, phone, address, plate or Stripe customer. Duplicate, fabricated or otherwise ineligible referrals do not qualify.

Refunds and payment reviews

A refunded or disputed referred order puts the reward on hold. Ineligible or cancelled referrals can be voided. A payment shown as pending or eligible is not a completed payout.

Payout and tax information

Provide an Interac delivery email for payment. Your legal name and address are required before a second payout. Additional information may be requested securely for tax reporting; Fabsy issues any required tax slips. You remain responsible for reporting your income.

Share honestly

Only describe results you actually experienced, make clear that you may earn a referral payment, and share with people who want the recommendation. Do not promise an outcome, impersonate Fabsy or send unsolicited bulk messages.

We show only referral status and payout information to the referrer, not the referred driver's case details. See Terms of Service section 5E and our Privacy Policy.

Referral questions

Who gets the referral reward, and how much is it?

The referrer receives $50 CAD for an eligible officer-ticket referral or $20 CAD for an eligible camera-ticket referral. The referred driver receives no referral discount, but may independently qualify for the verified pro driver discount.

When is a referral paid?

Fabsy pays approved rewards by manual Interac e-transfer seven days after both the referred Stripe payment has settled and the Alberta file has been accepted into the service pipeline. Eligibility checks and required payout details must be complete. Pending or eligible status does not mean money has been sent.

Do I need to be a past client, and is there a referral limit?

Past clients and registered Fabsy portal users can participate. A portal account is free and there is no cap on eligible referrals or total rewards.

How does Fabsy attribute a referral?

The most recent valid referral link or code captured before payment wins, within a 30-day window. The driver can enter the code at step 3 of intake if the share link was not captured.

Which referrals are excluded or held?

Referred fleet accounts and self-referrals are excluded. Matching email, phone, address, plate or Stripe customer blocks a payout. Refunded or disputed orders put the reward on hold, and an ineligible or cancelled referral can be voided.

What payout information is required?

An Interac delivery email is needed for payment. Your legal name and address are required before the second payout. Fabsy may request additional information securely for applicable tax reporting and issues any required tax slips. You remain responsible for reporting your income.
