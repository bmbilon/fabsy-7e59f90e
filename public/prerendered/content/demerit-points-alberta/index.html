Alberta Demerit Points: How Long They Last and Suspension Limits

You can expect Alberta demerit points to remain on your record for two years from conviction, with first suspension at 15 points for a full licence or 8 points for GDL.

Key facts

  • Demerit points are applied on the conviction date and remain on the record and abstract for 2 years.
  • A full-licence driver receives a first one-month suspension at 15 or more points within 2 years.
  • A GDL driver receives a first one-month suspension at 8 or more points within 2 years.
  • Cautionary notices start at 8 points for full licences and 4 points for GDL licences.
  • An approved driver training course may remove up to 3 points once every 2 years if timing requirements are met.

When demerit points are added and removed

Alberta applies demerit points on the conviction date, not the date the ticket was issued. A conviction date can be the day the ticket is paid, the day a court finds the driver guilty, or the day a conviction is entered after a failure to appear.

The points remain on the driving record and driver's abstract for two years from that conviction date. Full-licence drivers receive a cautionary notice at 8 to 14 points and a first one-month suspension at 15 or more; GDL drivers receive a warning at 4 to 7 and a first one-month suspension at 8 or more.

How to check and manage a demerit total

Obtain a current driver abstract from an Alberta registry agent and list each conviction date and point value. A pending ticket has not yet added its points, but its possible effect on the current total should be assessed before the ticket's response deadline.

An approved defensive driving or professional driver improvement course may remove up to three points once every two years. To avoid a suspension, Alberta requires the course to be completed before the conviction that causes the driver to reach the suspension threshold.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long do demerit points stay on an Alberta driving record?

Two years from conviction. Alberta removes the points from the driving record and driver's abstract two years after the conviction date, not the ticket issue date.

How many demerit points suspend a full Alberta licence?

Fifteen or more points within two years. A first demerit suspension for a fully licensed driver lasts one month under Alberta's current program.

How many demerit points suspend an Alberta GDL licence?

Eight or more points within two years. A first demerit suspension for a GDL driver lasts one month under Alberta's current program.

Does paying an Alberta traffic ticket add demerit points?

Yes, if the offence carries points. Alberta treats the payment date as the conviction date, which is when the points are applied to the driving record.

Can a driver training course reduce Alberta demerit points?

Yes. An approved course may remove up to three points once every two years, but Alberta's timing rules matter when a driver is close to suspension.
