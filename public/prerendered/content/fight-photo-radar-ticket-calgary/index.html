Calgary zones and approved exceptions

Alberta's default policy from April 1, 2025 restricts automated speed enforcement to school, playground and construction zones, with a process for approved exceptions. What Fabsy checks in disclosure

  • The vehicle and plate in the images match the notice.
  • The site was permitted, or had an approved exception, for the offence and date. Speed and red-light rules are checked separately.
  • The posted zone hours and any special signs applied at the recorded time.
  • The evidence supports workers being present if a doubled construction fine was applied.
  • Related notices are checked against the municipality's applicable duplicate-notice policy.
  • The certificate's mailing date and the applicable service requirements are checked.
  • Ownership on the offence date is checked against any sale or theft records.
  • Available operator logs and device testing or calibration records support the allegation.

Missing evidence is a reason to ask for it, not proof that the ticket is invalid. A supported concern becomes a fact-specific request to the Crown; it does not promise a reduction or withdrawal.
