Key facts

What a Calgary photo radar ticket means

A photo radar notice identifies the registered owner rather than a driver. It carries no demerits and does not appear on the driving abstract.

Since April 1, 2025, Alberta restricts photo radar to school, playground, and construction zones and prohibits it on provincial highways. Red-light cameras remain, while speed-on-green enforcement has ended.

How to review the notice

Read both sides of the notice and use the response deadline and instructions printed on it. Keep a copy and preserve any photographs, video, or documents that may be relevant.

If you choose to dispute the notice, request the available evidence and compare it with the allegation and location. The available response depends on the notice, the evidence, and the current court process.
