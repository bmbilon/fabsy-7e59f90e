What an Alberta camera notice means

The current Demerit Point Program and Service of Documents Regulation, Schedule 1 lists the offences that carry demerit points and does not list an owner contravention under Traffic Safety Act s.160. That legal point is not a guarantee about any insurer's underwriting, premium, or treatment of a particular record. A separate roadside ticket issued to an identified driver needs a different review.

This guide concerns automated notices mailed to a registered owner under Traffic Safety Act, ss.160 to 163, including photo radar speeding and red-light cameras. Edmonton explains owner liability. The notice can be disputed; follow its response instructions and the applicable Part 3 process.

Where photo radar is permitted

Alberta's default policy from April 1, 2025 restricts automated speed enforcement to school, playground and construction zones, with a process for approved exceptions. Red-light cameras have separate site rules. Check the site, offence type and approval that applied on the offence date. Read the provincial photo radar rules.

Designated school, playground and construction zones can still be permitted on provincial highways and connectors. A highway address alone is not a reason to treat a notice as invalid. See the provincial guideline, including exceptions.

Check the posted hours at the timestamp. Municipal playground-zone hours can differ from provincial school-zone defaults; a school holiday does not by itself end a playground-zone limit.

March 2026 fine schedule

Selected Alberta traffic fine increases took effect on March 13, 2026. The offence date determines which schedule applies. CPS explains the effective date.

Alberta's current standard speeding table lists $228 for 20 km/h over and $324 for 30 km/h over, including the 20% victim surcharge. Check the official speeding table. For a red-light notice, use the exact allegation and fine shown on the ticket, not the speeding table. Government fines are separate from Fabsy's service fee.

What Fabsy checks in disclosure

  • The vehicle and plate in the images match the notice.
  • The site was permitted, or had an approved exception, for the offence and date. Speed and red-light rules are checked separately.
  • The posted zone hours and any special signs applied at the recorded time.
  • If the higher construction-zone table was applied, the evidence supports the people-present circumstances in Traffic Safety Act, s.115(2)(p.2) and Procedures Regulation, Schedule, Part 33.
  • Related notices are checked against the municipality's applicable duplicate-notice policy.
  • The certificate's mailing date and the applicable service requirements are checked.
  • Ownership on the offence date is checked against any sale or theft records.
  • Available operator logs and device testing or calibration records support the allegation.

Missing evidence is a reason to ask for it, not proof that the ticket is invalid. A supported concern becomes a fact-specific request to the Crown; it does not promise a reduction or withdrawal.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does photo radar affect insurance in Alberta?

The current demerit schedule assigns no points for an owner conviction under Traffic Safety Act s.160. That is not a guarantee about an insurer's underwriting, premium, or treatment of a particular record. A separate roadside ticket issued to an identified driver is different.

Was photo radar made illegal in Alberta?

No. Automated speed enforcement is restricted to designated zones unless an exception is approved. Red-light cameras have different site rules. Approved exceptions exist, so a notice must be checked against its location, offence type and offence date.

Which fine schedule applies to a mailed camera ticket?

Selected Alberta traffic fine increases took effect on March 13, 2026. The offence date determines which schedule applies. Check the allegation and the applicable provincial schedule; the mailing date does not decide the fine.

Can duplicate camera notices be reviewed?

Edmonton says multiple notices must not be issued to the same vehicle within five minutes in the same municipality. For Calgary or another municipality, Fabsy checks the policy that applied to the notice before asking for duplicate-notice review.

Does missing disclosure automatically cancel my ticket?

No. A missing image, operator log, device record or mailing record requires follow-up. Fabsy separates missing information from a supported problem and asks the Crown for the appropriate review. You still need to meet the response and court deadlines.
