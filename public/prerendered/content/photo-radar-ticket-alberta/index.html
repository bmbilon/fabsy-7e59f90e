Photo Radar Tickets in Alberta Under the 2025 Rules

Photo radar tickets in Alberta go to the registered owner, carry no demerits, and do not appear on the driving abstract. Since April 1, 2025, photo radar is restricted to school, playground, and construction zones.

Key facts

  • Photo radar is banned on provincial highways and restricted to school, playground, and construction zones since April 1, 2025.
  • Tickets go to the registered owner and carry no demerits or driving-abstract entry.
  • Speed-on-green intersection enforcement is eliminated; red-light cameras remain.
  • The location, photographs, and ticket details are important facts to review before deciding how to respond.
  • The response deadline is printed on the ticket. Follow the ticket instructions before it.

What the 2025 rules changed

Alberta's automated traffic enforcement rules changed on April 1, 2025. Photo radar is prohibited on provincial highways and connectors and restricted to school, playground, and construction zones. Intersection cameras may still enforce red lights, but speed-on-green enforcement has ended.

A photo radar ticket identifies the registered owner rather than a driver. It carries no demerits and does not appear on the driving abstract.

How to respond to a photo radar ticket

  • Check the deadline printed on the ticket and respond before it.
  • Review the location. Confirm whether the ticket identifies a school, playground, or construction zone.
  • Review the photographs and vehicle details. Check that the plate and vehicle information match.
  • Follow the ticket instructions. Use the stated process to pay the ticket or start a dispute.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is photo radar still legal in Alberta?

Since April 1, 2025, photo radar is restricted to school, playground, and construction zones, and banned on provincial highways and connectors. Red-light cameras remain, but speed-on-green enforcement is eliminated.

Do photo radar tickets add demerits or appear on an Alberta driving abstract?

No. Photo radar tickets carry no demerit points and do not appear on the driving abstract.

Can you fight a photo radar ticket in Alberta?

Yes. Follow the dispute instructions and deadline printed on the ticket. The location, photographs, plate identification, and other ticket details are relevant facts to review.

Who has to pay a photo radar ticket?

The notice is issued to the registered owner. Owner-liability rules and statutory exceptions depend on the facts. Because no driver is identified, no demerits attach and the ticket does not appear on the driving abstract.

Is it worth fighting a photo radar ticket?

It depends on the amount and the facts. Photo radar tickets carry no demerits and do not appear on the driving abstract, so the decision is mainly about the fine and the evidence.

What changed with Alberta photo radar in 2025?

Effective April 1, 2025, Alberta prohibited photo radar on provincial highways and connectors, restricted its use to school, playground, and construction zones, and limited intersection safety devices to red-light enforcement.
