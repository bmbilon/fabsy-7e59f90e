Fight a Traffic Ticket in Edmonton

To fight a traffic ticket in Edmonton, enter a not guilty plea before the deadline printed on the ticket, request the Crown's disclosure, and resolve or contest the charge through the Alberta Court of Justice. An agent can appear on your behalf at every stage.

Key facts

  • The dispute deadline is printed on your ticket, typically about 30 days from issue.
  • Edmonton traffic matters are handled through the Alberta Court of Justice.
  • An agent can appear for you at first appearance and most hearings.
  • Disclosure (officer notes, device calibration, video) is where weak tickets fall apart.
  • Edmonton's photo radar program is now confined to school, playground, and construction zones.

What a conviction really costs

Edmonton drivers lose more to the conviction than the fine. Demerits accumulate toward suspension, and insurers reprice against convictions for three years: $1,000 to $3,000 in added premiums is a normal outcome for a single moving violation. Paying the ticket locks all of that in. Disputing it keeps reduction, withdrawal, and acquittal on the table.

Common Edmonton tickets we fight: speeding on the Henday, Whitemud, and Yellowhead, distracted driving, red-light camera tickets, careless driving, and photo radar from school and playground zones.

How it works

  • We file your not guilty plea with the court office listed on your ticket, before your deadline.
  • We pull disclosure: officer notes, radar and lidar records, calibration logs, camera evidence.
  • We negotiate or contest. Most matters end in a reduced charge or fine; where the evidence is weak we set the matter for trial and hold the Crown to its burden.
  • You stay out of court. We appear on your behalf for most Edmonton traffic matters.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I fight a traffic ticket in Edmonton?

Plead not guilty before the response deadline printed on the ticket, request disclosure, then negotiate a resolution or set the matter for trial through the Alberta Court of Justice in Edmonton. An agent can handle it all for you.

Do I have to appear in court in Edmonton?

Usually not. An authorized agent can attend first appearance and most hearings on your behalf. Personal attendance is generally only needed if you testify at trial.

What does an Edmonton traffic conviction cost?

The fine plus demerits on your abstract, plus a conviction your insurer sees for three years. Premium impacts of $1,000 to $3,000 over that window are common for moving violations.

Are Edmonton photo radar tickets worth fighting?

They carry no demerits and stay off your abstract, so it is purely about the fine. Under the April 2025 rules, sites outside school, playground, and construction zones are no longer legal, which strengthens many disputes.

Can I dispute an Edmonton ticket after the deadline?

Missing the deadline printed on the ticket normally means conviction by default. Limited relief exists in narrow circumstances, but do not count on it: act before the date on the ticket.
