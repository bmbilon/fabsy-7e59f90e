Edmonton Photo Radar Tickets Under Alberta's 2025 Rules

You can dispute an Edmonton photo radar ticket before its printed deadline; the notice goes to the registered owner and adds no demerit points to the operator's licence.

Key facts

  • Since April 1, 2025, Alberta restricts photo radar speed enforcement to school, playground, and construction zones.
  • Photo radar is prohibited on provincial highways and connectors under the 2025 rules.
  • The registered owner receives the Edmonton notice, with no demerits assessed to the operator's licence.
  • Edmonton's Safe Streets Map shows automated enforcement sites and published site assessments.
  • Edmonton states that the same vehicle must not receive multiple notices within 5 minutes in the municipality.

How Edmonton photo radar works now

Mobile automated speed enforcement in Edmonton operates under Alberta's April 1, 2025 guideline, which restricts photo radar to school, playground, and construction zones and prohibits it on provincial highways and connectors. Municipalities may submit exemption requests for qualifying high-collision sites.

An Edmonton notice is mailed to the registered owner with a vehicle photo, posted speed, violation speed, and fine amount. The City publishes automated enforcement locations and site assessments through its Safe Streets Map, while the provincial ticket system manages the response process after a notice is issued.

How to review and dispute an Edmonton notice

Review the full-sized colour images and every detail on the notice, including the plate, vehicle, location, time, posted speed, and alleged speed. Compare the location with Edmonton's Safe Streets Map and check whether a standard zone or approved exemption applies.

Follow the response instructions before the deadline printed on the notice if you choose to contest it. Preserve the images and request the available evidence; if the same vehicle received multiple Edmonton notices within five minutes, follow the City's published contact process.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where can photo radar operate in Edmonton?

In restricted zones. Alberta limits speed enforcement to school, playground, and construction zones, while municipalities may submit exemption requests for qualifying high-collision sites.

Do Edmonton photo radar tickets carry demerit points?

No. The notice goes to the registered owner of the vehicle, and Edmonton states that no demerit points are assessed to the operator's licence.

Can I review the images from an Edmonton photo radar ticket?

Yes. Edmonton provides online access to full-sized colour images, which can be compared with the plate, vehicle, location, time, and speed details on the notice.

Can one vehicle receive two Edmonton photo radar tickets within five minutes?

Generally, no. Edmonton states that multiple traffic notices must not be issued to the same vehicle within five minutes in the same municipality.

Can I dispute an Edmonton photo radar ticket?

Yes. Follow the provincial response instructions before the deadline printed on the notice, preserve the images, and review the location and evidence before contesting it.
