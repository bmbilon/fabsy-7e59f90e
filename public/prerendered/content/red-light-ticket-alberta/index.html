Alberta Red-Light Tickets: Officer-Issued and Camera Notices

An officer-issued Alberta red-light conviction carries three demerit points, while an automated notice is issued to the registered owner and does not add demerits to the operator's licence.

Key facts

  • An officer-issued red-light conviction carries three demerit points.
  • An automated red-light notice is an owner ticket and does not add driver demerits.
  • Alberta intersection safety devices are restricted to red-light enforcement.
  • The evidence and record consequences differ by ticket type.

Two kinds of Alberta red-light tickets

When an officer stops a vehicle and identifies the driver, a conviction for failing to stop at a red light carries three demerit points. When an intersection safety device records the vehicle, the notice goes to the registered owner and no demerits are assessed to the operator's licence.

As of December 2, 2024, Alberta restricts intersection safety devices to red-light enforcement. Municipalities were required to remove speed enforcement functionality from those devices.

How to assess the ticket

First confirm whether the ticket was issued by an officer or mailed after automated review. For an officer-issued ticket, disclosure may include notes and observations. For a camera notice, review the images, vehicle position, plate, signal sequence, location, and notice details.

Follow the response instructions and deadline printed on the ticket. Preserve relevant dashcam footage or photographs, request the available evidence, and compare the allegation with the intersection layout and circumstances before choosing a response.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many demerits does an officer-issued Alberta red-light ticket carry?

The current Alberta Driver's Guide assigns three demerit points for failing to stop for a red light at an intersection.

Does a red-light camera notice add demerits?

An automated red-light notice is issued to the registered owner and does not add demerit points to the operator's licence.

Can intersection cameras still issue speeding tickets?

Alberta states that, as of December 2, 2024, intersection safety devices can only be used at intersections to enforce red-light violations and must not retain speed-enforcement functionality.

What evidence should I review?

For an officer ticket, review the available notes and observations. For a camera notice, review the images, vehicle position, plate, signal sequence, location, and notice details.
