Red Light Tickets in Alberta: Officer and Camera Tickets

You can dispute an Alberta red light ticket before its printed deadline; an officer-issued conviction carries three demerits, while an automated owner ticket carries none.

Key facts

  • An officer-issued conviction for failing to stop at a red light carries 3 demerit points.
  • An automated red light notice goes to the registered owner and adds no demerits to the operator's licence.
  • Since December 2, 2024, intersection safety devices may enforce only red light violations.
  • Officer-issued and camera tickets rely on different evidence, so identify the ticket type first.
  • The response deadline and available options are printed on the ticket.

Two kinds of Alberta red light tickets

When an officer stops a vehicle and identifies the driver, a conviction for failing to stop at a red light carries three demerit points. When an intersection safety device records the vehicle, the notice goes to the registered owner and no demerits are assessed to the operator's licence.

Alberta restricted intersection safety devices to red light enforcement only as of December 2, 2024. Municipalities were required to remove speed enforcement functionality from those devices, while red light cameras remain in use.

How to assess and dispute the ticket

Start by confirming whether the ticket was issued by an officer or mailed after camera review. For an officer-issued ticket, disclosure may include notes and observations; for a camera ticket, review the images, signal sequence, vehicle position, plate, and notice details.

Follow the response instructions and deadline printed on the ticket. Preserve any dashcam footage or photographs, request the available evidence, and compare the allegation with the intersection layout and circumstances before choosing a resolution or trial.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many demerit points is a red light ticket in Alberta?

Three demerit points for an officer-issued red light conviction. An automated owner ticket does not add demerits to the operator's licence.

Do Alberta red light camera tickets carry demerit points?

No. The notice is issued to the registered owner of the vehicle, and no demerit points are assessed to the operator's licence.

Can Alberta intersection cameras issue speed-on-green tickets?

No. Since December 2, 2024, Alberta permits intersection safety devices to enforce red light violations only and requires municipalities to remove speed enforcement functionality.

Can I dispute a red light ticket in Alberta?

Yes. Follow the instructions on the ticket before its printed deadline, preserve relevant records, and review the officer or camera evidence supporting the allegation.

What evidence is used for an Alberta red light ticket?

It depends. Officer-issued tickets may rely on notes and observations, while automated owner tickets rely on reviewed images and details about the vehicle, signal, and intersection.
