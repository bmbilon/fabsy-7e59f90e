Fight a Traffic Ticket in Calgary

To fight a traffic ticket in Calgary, plead not guilty before the deadline printed on the ticket, request the Crown's evidence, and resolve or contest the charge through the Alberta Court of Justice. An agent can appear for you at every step.

Key facts

  • Your dispute deadline is printed on the ticket, typically about 30 days from issue.
  • Calgary traffic matters run through the Alberta Court of Justice at the Calgary Courts Centre.
  • An agent can attend first appearance and most hearings so you never miss work.
  • Disclosure review (officer notes, device records, video) is where most tickets get beaten.
  • Photo radar in Calgary is now limited to school, playground, and construction zones, with no demerits attached.

What you are really deciding

Paying a Calgary ticket is a guilty plea. The conviction lands on your abstract with its demerits, and your insurer prices against it for three years. Fighting it keeps every option open: charges get reduced, fines get cut, and weak cases get withdrawn once disclosure is on the table.

Common Calgary tickets we fight: speeding on Deerfoot, Glenmore, and Crowchild, distracted driving downtown, red-light and intersection camera tickets, careless driving, and photo radar from school and playground zones.

How it works

  • Before your deadline, we file your not guilty plea with the court office on your ticket.
  • We request disclosure: officer notes, radar or lidar calibration records, camera evidence.
  • We negotiate or contest. Most matters resolve with a reduced charge or fine; the rest we take to trial, where the Crown must prove every element beyond a reasonable doubt.
  • You get the outcome without courtroom mornings: we appear on your behalf for most Calgary traffic matters.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I fight a traffic ticket in Calgary?

Enter a not guilty plea before the response deadline printed on the ticket, request disclosure from the Crown, then resolve or go to trial at the Calgary Courts Centre. An agent can handle every appearance for you.

Where do Calgary traffic ticket disputes get heard?

Traffic matters for Calgary are handled through the Alberta Court of Justice at the Calgary Courts Centre. Your ticket lists the court office and the deadline that applies to you.

Do I need to show up in person in Calgary?

Usually not. An authorized agent can attend first appearance and most hearings on your behalf. You would only attend if your testimony is required at trial.

What does a conviction cost in Calgary?

Beyond the fine: demerits on your abstract and a conviction insurers see for three years. Premium increases of $1,000 to $3,000 over that period are common for moving violations.

Can Calgary photo radar tickets still be issued?

Only from school, playground, and construction zones under the province's April 2025 rules. Those tickets carry no demerits and never appear on your driving abstract.
