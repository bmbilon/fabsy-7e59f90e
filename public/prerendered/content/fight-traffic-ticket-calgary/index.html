Fight a Traffic Ticket in Calgary

To fight a traffic ticket in Calgary, follow the dispute instructions before the deadline printed on the ticket, request the available evidence, and decide whether to seek a resolution or proceed to trial. An agent may assist where representation is permitted.

Key facts

  • Use the dispute deadline printed on the ticket.
  • The ticket identifies the court office and response instructions that apply.
  • Attendance requirements depend on the charge, the court, and what happens in the matter.
  • Disclosure review helps identify the available response options.
  • Photo radar in Calgary is now limited to school, playground, and construction zones, with no demerits attached.

What you are really deciding

Paying a Calgary ticket is a guilty plea. The conviction lands on your abstract with any demerits. Disputing it keeps the available resolution and trial options open while the evidence is reviewed.

Common Calgary tickets we handle include speeding, distracted driving, red-light and intersection camera tickets, careless driving, and photo radar from school and playground zones.

How it works

  • Before your deadline, follow the response instructions on the ticket or ask an agent to assist where permitted.
  • Request disclosure, which may include officer notes, device records, or camera evidence.
  • Review the available path. A matter may resolve or may proceed to trial, depending on the allegation, evidence, and court process.
  • Confirm attendance requirements. They depend on the charge, the court, and what happens in the matter.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I fight a traffic ticket in Calgary?

Follow the dispute instructions before the response deadline printed on the ticket, request the available disclosure, and review whether the matter may resolve or proceed to trial. An agent may assist where representation is permitted.

Where do Calgary traffic ticket disputes get heard?

Use the court office and response instructions printed on your ticket. The applicable location and process depend on the notice and the matter.

Do I need to show up in person in Calgary?

Attendance requirements depend on the charge, the court, and what happens in the matter. An agent can tell you if personal attendance is required.

What does a conviction cost in Calgary?

Beyond the fine, a conviction can add demerits to your abstract. The effect depends on the offence and your existing driving record.

Can Calgary photo radar tickets still be issued?

Since April 1, 2025, Alberta restricts photo radar to school, playground, and construction zones. Those tickets carry no demerits and do not appear on the driving abstract.
