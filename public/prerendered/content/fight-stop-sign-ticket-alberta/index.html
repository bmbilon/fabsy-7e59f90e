Can You Fight an Alberta Stop Sign Ticket?

You can dispute an Alberta stop sign ticket by following the response instructions before the deadline printed on the ticket and reviewing the available evidence.

Key facts

  • Use the response deadline and instructions printed on the ticket.
  • Keep the ticket and any relevant photographs, video, or documents.
  • Request the available evidence before deciding how to proceed.
  • The available response depends on the allegation, the evidence, and the court process.

What disputing a stop sign ticket means

Disputing a ticket starts with the notice itself. Confirm the alleged offence, location, court office, and response deadline printed on the ticket. Keep the original and any records related to the event.

The available response depends on the allegation, the evidence, and the current court process. No service can predict the result of an individual matter.

How to review your options

Follow the response instructions before the deadline printed on the ticket. Request the available disclosure and review it alongside any relevant photographs, video, documents, or witness information.

A matter may resolve or may proceed to trial. Attendance requirements depend on the charge, the court, and what happens in the matter.
