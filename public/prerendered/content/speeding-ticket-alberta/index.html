Speeding Tickets in Alberta: What They Cost and How to Fight Them

An officer-issued speeding ticket in Alberta carries two to six demerit points depending on how far over the limit you were and can be disputed by pleading not guilty before the deadline printed on the ticket.

Key facts

  • Demerits scale with speed: 2 (up to 15 over), 3 (16 to 30), 4 (31 to 50), 6 (over 50).
  • More than 50 km/h over the limit means a mandatory court appearance.
  • Photo radar speeding tickets carry no demerits and never touch your abstract; officer-issued tickets do.
  • Disclosure may include officer notes and records about the speed-measuring device.
  • The available response depends on the allegation, evidence, and circumstances.

What a speeding conviction means

A conviction can add demerits toward suspension. Suspension starts at 15 demerits for a full licence and 8 for a GDL. The effect depends on the alleged speed and your existing driving record.

Speed measurement may depend on the device records, operator notes, and conditions described in the disclosure. Reviewing that evidence helps determine the available response.

How to dispute a speeding ticket

  • Check the ticket. Use the response deadline and instructions printed on it.
  • Keep your records. Preserve the ticket, photographs, video, and your written account of the event.
  • Request available disclosure. Review the allegation, officer notes, and any device records provided.
  • Choose a response. The available path depends on the allegation, evidence, court process, and circumstances.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many demerits is a speeding ticket in Alberta?

Two demerits up to 15 km/h over, three for 16 to 30 over, four for 31 to 50 over, and six for more than 50 over. More than 50 over also means a mandatory court appearance.

Should I pay my speeding ticket or fight it?

Paying is a conviction and can add demerits to your driving record. Review the ticket and your options before the deadline printed on it.

How do I dispute a speeding ticket in Alberta?

Follow the dispute instructions before the response deadline printed on the ticket. Request the available disclosure and review the allegation, officer notes, and device records before choosing how to proceed.

Do photo radar speeding tickets add demerits?

No. Photo radar tickets go to the registered owner, carry no demerits, and do not appear on your driving abstract. Officer-issued speeding tickets do carry demerits.

Can a speeding charge be reduced?

A charge may be amended or resolved, but the available outcome depends on the allegation, the evidence, and the circumstances. Disclosure review helps identify the available options.
