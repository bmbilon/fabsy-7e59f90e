Careless Driving Tickets in Alberta: Demerits and Options

You can dispute an Alberta careless driving ticket before the printed deadline, and a conviction adds six demerit points to your driving record.

Key facts

  • Alberta assigns 6 demerit points for a careless driving conviction.
  • Demerit points are applied on the conviction date and remain on the record and abstract for 2 years.
  • The surrounding driving conduct and circumstances matter when the charge is assessed.
  • Officer observations, video, witness accounts, vehicle movements, and road conditions may be relevant evidence.
  • A first demerit suspension starts at 15 points for a full licence or 8 points for a GDL licence.

What a careless driving ticket means

Alberta lists careless driving as a six-demerit offence. Unlike a rule focused on one device or traffic signal, the allegation concerns the manner of driving, so the complete event and surrounding circumstances matter.

Relevant material may include the officer's observations, video, witness accounts, vehicle movements, traffic, visibility, weather, and road conditions. The charge should be assessed against the conduct and evidence alleged on the ticket, not assumptions based only on its label.

How to assess and dispute the charge

Check the response deadline printed on the ticket and write down a precise chronology while the event is fresh. Preserve dashcam footage, photographs, witness details, and information about traffic, visibility, weather, and the road.

If you dispute the charge, request disclosure and review the officer's notes and any other evidence the Crown intends to rely on. Compare that material with your records before deciding whether to seek a resolution or proceed to trial.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many demerit points is careless driving in Alberta?

Six demerit points. Alberta applies them on the conviction date, and they remain on the driving record and driver's abstract for two years from that date.

Will one careless driving conviction suspend my licence?

Not necessarily. A six-point conviction does not alone reach either suspension threshold, but existing points may bring the total to 15 on a full licence or 8 on a GDL.

Can a careless driving ticket in Alberta be disputed?

Yes. Follow the instructions on the ticket before its printed deadline, preserve relevant records, and request disclosure if you choose to contest the charge.

What evidence matters for a careless driving ticket?

It depends. Officer notes, video, witness accounts, vehicle movements, traffic, visibility, weather, and road conditions can help establish what happened and how the allegation should be assessed.

Does a careless driving ticket add demerits immediately?

No. Alberta applies the six points on the conviction date, which may be the payment date, the date of a guilty finding, or a conviction after failure to appear.
