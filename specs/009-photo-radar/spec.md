# Rapid Resolution: Photo Radar

## Constitution

Preserve existing Rapid Resolution, free ticket check, insurance report, multilingual release gates, private intake, and pending user work. Owner-liability automated notices are a separate product with no demerits, no insurance impact, no insurance report upsell, no trial, and no success fee. Describe actions Fabsy controls; never promise a reduction, withdrawal, response time from the Crown, or an automatic legal defence. Verify legal statements against current Alberta and municipal primary sources. Clients approve every Crown agreement through the existing offer flow.

## Specification

Add Rapid Resolution: Photo Radar for Alberta automated enforcement notices mailed to the registered owner under Traffic Safety Act s.160(1), including photo radar speeding and red-light cameras. Charge CAD 79 plus 5% GST (CAD 82.95 total). Enter the not-guilty plea, request disclosure, review complete disclosure and prepare or take the next authorized Crown step within 48 hours. Pursue a reduction or withdrawal; no trial or outcome-based refund.

Add `/photo-radar` with the requested headline, three-step process, six visible FAQs sharing their source with FAQPage JSON-LD, and a Service offer at CAD 79. Keep and refresh the Alberta, Edmonton and Calgary photo radar content pages, linking them to the new product. Add `/fleet` for delivery, rental, trades and dealers: one intake for all plates, CAD 79 per ticket, account pricing at 5+ per month and monthly QuickBooks invoicing arranged with Fabsy.

The intake identifies owner notices from upload evidence, allows manual correction and asks ownership on the offence date (yes, sold before, stolen). Skip insurance context and hide insurance upsells for owner notices. Persist the type and ownership answer; select the SKU and ATE pipeline on the server, not by trusting an amount from the browser. Preserve payment idempotency, authorization and webhook verification.

The ATE review records plate/image match, permitted site/exception, active zone hours, workers for doubled construction fines, city-specific five-minute duplicate policy, certificate mailing and applicable service timing, ownership, and operator/calibration disclosure. Missing or adverse evidence creates a review question/Crown request, not a fabricated finding or guaranteed withdrawal. Track Crown fine reduction on the first 20 ATE files, including zero reduction, with a median and the under-CAD-40 decision threshold.

Update pricing ladder, Terms 5C, homepage, services, AI info, footer, priceRange, snapshots, sitemap and llms.txt. Prepare exact/phrase Google Search and Meta creative with eligibility as the hook; measure paid customer CAC, scaling below CAD 35 and stopping at CAD 55 subject to an approved spend budget.

## Clarifications before plan

The build sheet resolves the core scope; no implementation-blocking question is required. Existing intake has more than three screens, so adapt its current architecture while removing insurance-only questions for this product. Keep personally identifying intake and consent requirements where needed. Sold/stolen answers are review flags, not automatic denials. A red-light camera must not inherit speed-only zone restrictions. Apply legal rules by offence date and jurisdiction. English is the release language; do not publish unreviewed translations. Fleet account pricing is negotiated, not an invented discount or an unconnected invoicing promise. External account access, billing approval and actual campaign budget are launch dependencies and do not block the local build.
