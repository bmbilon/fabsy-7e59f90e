## Prominent message

> Fine or demerits reduced—or your fee refunded. Service fee paid upfront. If the Crown rejects Fabsy's efforts to reduce your original fine or demerits or withdraw your ticket, and none of those improvements is obtained, Fabsy refunds the service fee and GST actually paid. The refund is issued within 30 days of Fabsy receiving the Crown's rejection. Payment or an initial unchanged offer does not start the clock. No legal outcome is guaranteed. See website for details.

This guarantees the fee refund under the stated condition, not a Crown or court outcome. Do not substitute “you don't pay”: checkout collects the fee upfront. Do not advertise an unqualified “success guaranteed.” The comparison is against the original fine and original demerits; a reduction in either, or withdrawal, means the no-improvement condition is not met. The Crown must reject Fabsy's negotiation efforts before the refund clock starts.

The amount is the **full service fee and GST actually paid**, including the full Rapid Resolution/report bundled fee and GST after any Pro discount. Do not limit the bundle refund to its ticket component or pay an extra undiscounted amount on top of a Pro adjustment. Government fines are separate. Standalone Insurance Impact & Renewal Planning Report purchases are excluded.

The 30 days start when **Fabsy receives the Crown's rejection of its negotiation efforts**, with no reduction or withdrawal obtained. They do not start at payment, checkout or an initial/unchanged offer received before that rejection. There is no added final-offer requirement, minimum reduction, customer claim deadline, or requirement to accept an offer or guilty plea. Do not introduce any of these through later copy variations or footnotes.

Photo Radar keeps the short headline **“Fine reduced—or your fee refunded”** and includes withdrawal in its condition. If the Crown rejects Fabsy's negotiations for a lower original fine or withdrawal, and neither is obtained, Fabsy refunds the actual service fee and GST within 30 days of receiving that rejection. Owner camera notices have no demerits; an existing zero-demerit position is not a reduction. No insurance result is promised.
