## Prominent message

> Fine or demerits reduced—or your fee refunded. Service fee paid upfront. If a Crown offer reduces neither your original fine nor your original demerits, Fabsy refunds your service fee within 30 days of receiving that offer. No legal outcome is guaranteed. See website for details.

This guarantees the fee refund under the stated condition, not a Crown or court outcome. Do not substitute “you don't pay”: checkout collects the fee upfront. Do not advertise an unqualified “success guaranteed.” The comparison is against the original fine and original demerits; a reduction in either means the no-improvement condition is not met.

The amount is the **full service fee actually paid**, including the full Rapid Resolution/report bundled fee after any Pro discount. Do not limit the bundle refund to its ticket component or pay an extra undiscounted amount on top of a Pro adjustment. Government fines are separate. Standalone Insurance Impact & Renewal Planning Report purchases are excluded.

The 30 days start when **Fabsy receives the Crown offer**. There is no added final-offer requirement, minimum reduction, customer claim deadline, or requirement to accept an offer or guilty plea. Do not introduce any of these through later copy variations or footnotes.

Photo Radar uses **“Fine reduced—or your fee refunded.”** If a Crown offer does not reduce the original fine, Fabsy refunds the actual service fee within 30 days of receiving that offer. Owner camera notices have no demerits; an existing zero-demerit position is not a reduction. No insurance result is promised.

## Supporting benefit and scope
