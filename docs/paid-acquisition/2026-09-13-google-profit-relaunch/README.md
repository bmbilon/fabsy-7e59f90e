# Google Search profit relaunch — September 13, 2026

This relaunch uses the only campaign with live search history as a tightly bounded validation campaign. The previous September 2–11 run spent **CA$62.87** for 11 clicks and zero reported conversions. Seven of those clicks were exposed in the search-term report; two costly clicks came from the unrelated query `demerit reduction course alberta`, and several impressions came from government/payment navigation queries.

## Launch configuration

- Campaign: `RR-Search-Alberta-202609` (existing campaign, extended through September 27)
- Location: Alberta; people in or regularly in the location
- Network: Google Search only
- Language/ad groups: English only for this validation window
- Budget: CA$10 average daily budget
- Bidding: Maximize clicks with a CA$3.25 maximum CPC bid
- Final URL: `https://fabsy.ca/rapid-resolution?utm_source=google&utm_medium=cpc&utm_campaign=rr_google_profit_20260913&utm_content=en_rsa_v1&utm_term=traffic_ticket_defense`
- Primary reporting actions: paid Rapid Resolution, completed ticket upload, and qualified 60-second calls
- End date: September 27, 2026

The CA$3.25 click ceiling cuts the previous CA$5.72 average CPC by about 43%. At the CA$198 service price, gross revenue covers ad spend at approximately one sale per 61 clicks before service costs, GST, refunds, and overhead. The CA$10 average daily budget limits the nominal 15-day test spend to about CA$150; Google may spend more than the average on an individual day under its standard budget rules.

## Intent controls

The campaign retains Alberta traffic-ticket defense exact and phrase terms. It excludes observed government navigation, payment, course, camera-ticket, employment, and free-service intent. New negatives from the live query report are:

- `"traffic tickets alberta ca"`
- `"traffictickets alberta ca"`
- `ttds`
- `"fine payment"`
- `"fine payments"`
- `"pay online"`
- `"court of justice"`
- `"camera ticket"`
- `"speeding camera ticket"`

## Decision rule

Keep the campaign on click bidding until paid landings, uploads, calls, and purchases can be reconciled. Do not treat CTR as profit. Evaluate search terms and the first-party acquisition report after meaningful traffic arrives. Scale only from verified paid customers; pause or tighten the campaign if queries drift or the acquisition cost exceeds the service's contribution margin.
