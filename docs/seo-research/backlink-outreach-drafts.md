## Outreach rules

- Identify Fabsy as a **for-profit Alberta traffic-ticket service** in the first message.
- Ask for independent assessment under the recipient's editorial/resource criteria, not an endorsement.
- Never offer money, reciprocal links, reviews, client referrals, donations, or sponsorship in exchange for a link.
- Never ask a clinic or settlement organization to steer clients away from free help.
- Do not use phrases such as “partner of,” “approved by,” “trusted by,” or “recommended by” unless a separate written agreement expressly permits the exact statement.
- Send one tailored message. If there is no response, send at most one short follow-up after 10 business days, then stop.
- Use a plain canonical URL without tracking parameters. Do not attach large files.
- Record corrections even if the organization declines to link. Accuracy is the first return on this outreach.
- Re-open both the relevant page and contact page immediately before sending; roles, scope, and policies change.
