# Ways to Respond to an Alberta Traffic Ticket

An Alberta traffic ticket can usually be handled by responding yourself, hiring an authorized agent where permitted, or consulting a lawyer when the matter requires legal advice or falls outside an agent's permitted scope.

## Start with the ticket

Read the notice carefully. Confirm the alleged offence, court location, and response deadline printed on the ticket. Keep the original and any related records. Use current Alberta government and Alberta Court of Justice information when checking the process.

The best response depends on the allegation, available evidence, driving record, and desired outcome. No service can predict the result of an individual matter.

## Compare the available paths

### Respond on your own

Self-representation may suit someone who is comfortable following the ticket instructions, requesting available material, tracking dates, and appearing when required.

### Use an authorized agent

An authorized agent may review the ticket and disclosure, handle paperwork, explain available options, and provide representation where Alberta rules permit. Ask any provider to explain its status, scope, pricing, communication process, and any attendance requirement.

### Consult a lawyer

A lawyer may be appropriate when legal advice is needed or a matter is outside an agent's permitted scope. Ask about scope and fees before retaining anyone.

## Questions to ask a provider

1. What services are included?
2. Is representation permitted for this charge and court location?
3. What is the complete pricing formula?
4. Will personal attendance be required?
5. How are updates delivered?
