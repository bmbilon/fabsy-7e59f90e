Ontario provincial offences

Traffic Tickets

A ticket comes with a deadline and a decision to make. We can review the notice and talk through your next step.

Start with the notice.

Tell us what happened and have a copy of your Ontario offence notice ready. We will check the type of notice, the date on it and whether the matter is within the services our paralegal can provide.

Do not ignore a response date while waiting for a consultation. The date and options printed on your notice remain important.

What happens next?

  • We review the notice and your questions.
  • We explain what work we can take on and any proposed fee.
  • If you decide to proceed, we set out the scope in a written retainer.
Ontario traffic matters begin with a consultation. Please call or email us about the notice and its deadline.
