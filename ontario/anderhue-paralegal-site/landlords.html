Rules update

What changed in 2026

7-day N4, new form, half-arrears rule, N12 at 120 days.

Current to September 25, 2026.

  1. Sep 21N4 notice: 7 days, down from 14. Monthly and yearly tenancies. Mail still adds five days.
  2. Sep 21New N4 form (2026/09). The 2022 form no longer works.
  3. Sep 21Half the arrears first. Tenants must pay half before raising unrelated issues like repairs.
  4. Sep 21N12 at 120 days. No compensation owed with at least 120 days’ notice.
  5. Sep 21Persistent late payment defined. The Act now sets the test for N8 and L2.
  6. Jul 1Reviews: 15 days. Down from 30 to ask the Board to review an order.

Questions

What landlords ask us

Paying to void an N4, older notices, hearings, exclusions.

Can the tenant cancel the N4 by paying?

Yes. Full payment of the arrears before the L1 is filed voids the N4. After filing, the tenant still has limited ways to stop the eviction by paying what is owed plus your filing fee.

My N4 was served before September 21. Is it still good?

It follows the rules in force when served: 14 days for monthly and yearly tenancies, on the form that applied then. We check it before you file.

Do I need to be at the hearing?

Usually, yes. The adjudicator may need to hear from you about the rent owed. We prepare you and represent you.

What doesn’t the flat fee cover?

HST, the LTB filing fee, adjournments, second hearings, review requests and Sheriff enforcement. Anything extra is quoted in writing first.
