What we collect

When you contact us or start a file, we collect:

  • Contact details, such as your name, email address and phone number.
  • Details about your matter that you share with us, such as dates, amounts and what happened.
  • Documents you upload, such as notices, tickets, leases, rent ledgers, court papers and photo ID.

Why we collect it

We use your information to reply to you, to assess your matter and, if you retain us, to represent you.

Who can see it

The practice’s licensee and staff can see your information. So can the service providers we use for hosting, email and document reading, which may process it outside Canada.

How software reads your documents

Software reads the documents you upload to set up your file, so you type less. A licensee confirms anything it reads before we rely on it.

Secure links

Our emails include secure links to your file. They expire after 30 days, and you can ask for a new one at any time on the Your file page.

A link opens your file for whoever has it, until it expires. Please do not forward these emails.

How long we keep it

We keep files for the periods the Law Society of Ontario requires.

Questions and corrections

To ask about, correct or withdraw your information, email hello@anderhue.ca. You can also call (289) 985-0166, Mon–Fri, 9–5.
