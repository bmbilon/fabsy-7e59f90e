How it works

How your file moves forward.

The same four steps for every file, so you always know where things stand and what comes next.

  1. Send your documents securely

    From your phone or computer. Photos and PDFs both work, and you can add more later.

  2. We review and quote a fee in writing

    A licensed paralegal reviews your documents and dates, then tells you what the work involves and what it costs.

  3. You sign a retainer and we get to work

    Representation begins only after you sign a written retainer setting out the work and the fee.

  4. Follow every step online

    Your file shows where things stand and what happens next, with an email at each stage.

Your file, online
