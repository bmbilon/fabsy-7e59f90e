Services

What we can help with

Every matter is different. We look at your documents and dates first, then confirm in writing what we can do and what it costs.

  • Small Claims Court

    Help with a claim you want to bring, or a claim made against you.

  • Tribunals and boards

    Matters before Ontario tribunals and boards where a licensed paralegal can act for you.

  • Other provincial offences

    Offence notices and summonses under other Ontario laws, beyond traffic tickets.

  • Commissioning and notarizing

    Affidavits and declarations commissioned, and documents notarized, by a Notary Public and Commissioner of Oaths.

If another professional is better placed to help, we will say so.

Some matters need a lawyer, or a different kind of help. We tell you early, so you can find the right person while there is still time.

How it works

What happens after you get in touch.

You can follow each step in your file online, with an email when it moves forward.

  1. Tell us about the matter

    Describe what happened and upload any notice, claim or letter you have.

  2. We confirm whether we can help

    We review the matter and any dates that apply, then quote a fee in writing.

  3. You sign a retainer

    Representation begins only after you sign a written retainer setting out the work and the fee.

  4. Follow your file online

    See where things stand and what happens next, with an email at each stage.

Watch your deadline. If a document gives you a response or filing deadline, do not wait for our reply before taking steps to protect it. Tell us the date when you start your file.
