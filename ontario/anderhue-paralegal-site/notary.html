How Don can help

Get the right service for your document.

  • Notarization and certified copies: ask about witnessing a signature or certifying a copy of an original document.
  • Affidavits and declarations: ask about taking an oath, affirmation or declaration before a Commissioner of Oaths.

Include any instructions from the organization requesting your document so Don can check what is required. If you are unsure which service you need, describe the document in your request.

Don Anderson is a Paralegal licensed by the Law Society of Ontario, a Notary Public and a Commissioner of Oaths.

Confirm the details before your appointment.

You can upload the document securely with your request, or start without an upload. Tell us when it is needed and how to reach you.

We will confirm the fee in writing, appointment arrangements and which original documents and identification to bring. Submitting a request does not book an appointment or complete the notarization or commissioning.
